Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

In short

  • Your travel history stays on your phone. Nobody else can see it, including us.
  • Talyy has no account, no ads and no tracking across other apps.
  • If you allow it, Talyy shares crash reports and general usage statistics to help us fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn this off at any time.

Talyy is made by Guillermo Garcia, an independent developer. This policy explains what Talyy stores, what it shares, and the choices you have.

Your travel history

The days, countries, limits, trips and tickets you record are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Talyy has no server of its own, so this information never leaves your phone.

If you delete Talyy or lose your phone, this information is gone, unless your phone's own backup includes it.

Help improve Talyy

If you allow it, Talyy uses Google Firebase to collect crash reports and general usage statistics. We see them only as totals and trends across all users, never as your personal records. They cover:

  • How the app is used, in general terms, such as which features are opened.
  • App settings and totals, such as your chosen language, or how many countries you have recorded in all.
  • Crash reports, with any dates and numbers removed.
  • Technical information, such as the app version, phone model, iOS or Android version, a random identifier created by Firebase, and the approximate location the app is used from.

They never include the countries, dates, trips or tickets you record, anything you type, your name or your email.

Your choice

In the European Union and the European Economic Area, and on phones with no region set, Talyy asks first and shares nothing until you agree. In the United Kingdom and Switzerland, sharing is on by default, and Talyy shows you a notice and shares nothing until you have seen it. Elsewhere, sharing is on by default.

You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings, under Help improve Talyy. Turning it off stops all sharing and deletes the Firebase identifier and any unsent crash reports from your phone.

Legal basis

Where the law requires consent, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Elsewhere, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

Who receives the data

Google provides Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to us as a service provider. Google may process the data in the United States and other countries, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • Usage statistics are kept for 14 months.
  • Google keeps crash reports for 90 days before deleting them.
  • Google states that it does not log or store the internet addresses of users in the EU, the UK and Switzerland.

Google describes its own practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you test Talyy through TestFlight, Apple also shares crash logs, usage information and any feedback you send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because Talyy has no account, the statistics are not linked to your name or email. The quickest way to stop and remove them is the switch in Settings. For anything else, write to us.

Children

Talyy is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new date. If a change is significant, for example if Talyy would share something new, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and ask again where your consent is needed.
